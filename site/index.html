How this page is built

  • {{ c }}
    •

Every block comes from Justin's recordings of the sessions he attended: all five keynotes, both Saturday panels, and Education and STEM track sessions on Thursday and Friday. The conference also ran business, church, healthcare and arts tracks that are not covered here except where the track leaders summarized them on Saturday.

The recordings were turned into text by automatic captioning, which garbles names and some passages. Quotes were limited to passages that came through cleanly, and each one was checked by script against the transcript, word for word. Figures were checked against the transcript one at a time. Speaker names were corrected against the published program where it listed them.

The Ask the Conference page is different. It sends the blocks and your question to a language model and shows the answer.
